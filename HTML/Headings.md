# Headings

## 📖 Definition

Headings are titles and subtitles on a page. HTML has six heading levels, from `<h1>` to `<h6>`.

## 🤔 Why Do We Use It?

They organize a page, help readers scan it, and help screen readers understand its outline.

## 🧠 Simple Explanation

An `<h1>` is the main title of a book. `<h2>` is a chapter title, and `<h3>` is a smaller section inside that chapter.

## 📝 Syntax

```html
<h1>Chocolate Cake</h1>
<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<h3>For the cake</h3>
```

Use headings in order; choose a level for meaning, not only for its size.
