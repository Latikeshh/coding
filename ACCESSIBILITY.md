# Accessibility Statement

**Coding** is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We continuously improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards.

---

## ♿ Measures to Support Accessibility

We take the following measures to ensure accessibility across our educational hub:

- **Keyboard Navigation**: Full keyboard navigation support across the site and search engine (`Ctrl+K`, `Tab`, `Arrow Keys`, `Enter`, `Esc`).
- **Color Contrast**: Enriched dark theme with high contrast text ratios exceeding WCAG 2.1 AA standards for optimal readability.
- **Semantic HTML5**: Native semantic elements (`<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<aside>`, `<article>`, `<button>`) with proper ARIA attributes.
- **Screen Reader Compatibility**: Screen reader friendly navigation labels and alt tags on icons.
- **Responsive Layout**: Fluid responsive design that scales seamlessly across all viewport sizes and zoom levels up to 200%.

---

## 💬 Feedback & Reporting Barriers

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of the **Coding** repository website. If you encounter accessibility barriers:

- **GitHub Issue**: Open an issue labeled `accessibility` on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Latikeshh/coding/issues).
- **Email**: Email the maintainer directly at **[latikesh29@gmail.com](mailto:latikesh29@gmail.com)**.

We try to respond to accessibility feedback within **24–48 hours**.
