03. Sticky Headers & z-index Stacking Contexts

🟡 Intermediate

📖 Definition

position: sticky acts like relative positioning until a scroll threshold is met, whereupon it sticks like fixed positioning. z-index controls the 3D stacking order of overlapping positioned elements.
