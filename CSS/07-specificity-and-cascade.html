07. Specificity Scoring, Cascade & Inheritance

🟡 Intermediate

📖 Definition

The Cascade resolves conflicts between competing CSS rules based on Origin, Specificity score (Inline, ID, Class, Element), and Order of Appearance (the last rule declared wins on equal scores).
